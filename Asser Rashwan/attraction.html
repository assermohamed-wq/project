Explore the Wonders of Egypt

Discover the most famous attractions in Egypt.

Pyramids of Giza

The Pyramids of Giza are one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, located near Cairo.

Luxor Temple

Luxor Temple is a majestic ancient Egyptian temple located on the banks of the Nile River.

Valley of the Kings

Home to the tombs of many pharaohs, including the famous tomb of King Tutankhamun.

Abu Simbel

The Abu Simbel temples are massive rock temples built by Pharaoh Ramesses II, located near the Sudanese border.
