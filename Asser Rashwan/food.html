Famous Egyptian Foods

Explore some of the most popular and traditional dishes from Egypt.

Traditional Egyptian Dishes

Koshari

Koshari is a staple Egyptian comfort food, made with rice, lentils, macaroni, topped with crispy onions and tomato sauce.

Fattah

Fattah is a festive dish of rice, meat (usually lamb or beef), and crispy bread, often enjoyed during holidays and celebrations.

Molokhia

Molokhia is a thick, green stew made from the leaves of the jute plant, served with rice or bread, and often paired with chicken or rabbit.

Falafel

Known as "Ta'ameya" in Egypt, falafel is a deep-fried ball made from ground fava beans, herbs, and spices. It's typically served in pita bread with salad and tahini sauce.

Mahshi

Mahshi is a dish of stuffed vegetables such as zucchini, eggplant, and grape leaves, filled with rice, herbs, and minced meat.

Shawarma

Shawarma is a popular street food made from marinated meat (often chicken or beef), cooked on a vertical rotisserie, and served in pita bread with vegetables and sauces.
